# ADR NNNN: Decision title

- Status: Proposed
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD
- Owners: Product / Architecture / Engineering / Design, as applicable
- Supersedes: None

## Context

Describe the business need, current constraints, measurable problem, and trust or operational boundaries. Link evidence instead of relying on preference.

## Decision

State one decision in testable terms. Identify the system of record, owning module, public interface, and prohibited alternatives.

## Alternatives considered

Record credible alternatives and why they were not selected. Include total cost of ownership, failure behavior, reversibility, and team capacity.

## Consequences

List positive and negative consequences, new operational responsibilities, security impact, and any constraints this creates.

## Rollout

Define incremental steps, compatibility requirements, validation, observability, and the condition for completing the migration.

## Rollback

Define the safe reversal point, data handling, and owner. A rollback that loses accepted orders is not safe.

## Verification

List automated checks and runtime evidence proving the decision is correctly implemented.
